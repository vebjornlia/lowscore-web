Build the Lowscore shortcut once

About five minutes on your iPhone. After this, flatmates only install it and make one automation. Menu names below are the English ones; the Norwegian ones are in brackets where I know them.

Part 1 · Get your link

  1. Open the join page on your iPhone, pick a name, and tap Copy link 1 (Kopier lenke 1).
  2. Paste it in the box below and copy the converted link. It is the same link with the last word changed from open- to app-.
Nothing pasted yet.

The link holds your personal code. It stays on this page; nothing is sent anywhere.

Part 2 · Build it

  1. Open Shortcuts (Snarveier), tap +, and name the shortcut Lowscore.
  2. Add the action Text (Tekst) and paste the converted link into it.
  3. Add the action Get Current App. It needs iOS 18.2 or newer.
  4. Add Get Contents of URL (Hent innhold på URL). In its URL field, insert the variable Text and, right after it, the variable Current App. Nothing else, no spaces.
  5. Tap Done (Ferdig).

Part 3 · Test it on your own phone

  1. In Shortcuts: Automation › + › App › Choose: the apps you have from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, Facebook, X, Reddit, Twitch.
  2. Tick both Is Opened and Is Closed. Choose Run Immediately and turn Notify When Run off. Tap Next and pick Lowscore from the list.
  3. Open Instagram for half a minute. If iPhone asks whether Lowscore may connect, choose Always Allow. Then go to the home screen.
  4. Open the join page again. The bar at the bottom should say the board saw Instagram open, and then close.

If the open shows but the close never does, Get Current App fails on the home screen. Tell Claude, and we fall back to two automations.

Part 4 · Make it shareable

  1. Open the Lowscore shortcut for editing, tap the i at the bottom, then Setup. Add an import question for the Text action with the question “Paste your Lowscore link”. That way your own link is not shared and each person is asked for theirs.
  2. Tap Share › Copy iCloud Link. Send the link to yourself (mail or a note) and paste it into the Claude chat, or tell Claude you need another way.

I haven't been able to check on a phone exactly where Setup sits in your iOS version. If you can't find it, send the link anyway and say so: before sharing, clear the Text action so your own link isn't in it.
