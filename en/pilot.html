The Bergen pilot

In January 2027, five flats in Bergen each borrow an e-paper board for four weeks. It's free, and you hand it back afterwards. The boards are hand-built prototypes, so something will go wrong. That's why we need you.

How it goes

  1. 1

    We visit between 4 and 8 January, put up the board and help everyone who wants to play get started. About half an hour.

  2. 2

    In the first week the board finds each player's par. Then you play until Sunday 31 January.

  3. 3

    Each week you answer a short check-in, about three minutes. Honest answers, including what's annoying.

  4. 4

    In February we pick up the board and have a half-hour chat. The pilot data is deleted by 2 March 2027 at the latest.

Who we're looking for

  • A shared flat in Bergen, or a family with teenagers (13 and over)
  • 3 to 8 people sharing a kitchen, ideally a mix of iPhone and Android
  • Wi-Fi in the kitchen, and a fridge a magnet sticks to
  • That everyone who plays wants to. Each person decides for themselves, and can leave whenever they like.
