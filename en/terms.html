Lowscore terms of use and sale

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

  • Lowscore is a game for households. The board shows who scrolled least this week, measured against their own normal level.
  • Everyone who plays signs up themselves, from their own phone. Lowscore must never be used to monitor anyone.
  • You must be at least 13 to play, and 18 to create a board or buy.
  • The first season (4 weeks) is free. After that the flat pass costs NOK 149 for half a year, for the whole household. It does not renew by itself.
  • You have a 14-day right of withdrawal. If you withdraw, you get all your money back, even if the pass has started.
  • If you want to stop before the pass runs out, you get back the whole months that are left.
  • The numbers are estimates. Lowscore is a game, not a measuring instrument or a treatment.
  • Consumer law always applies. Nothing here takes away rights the law gives you.

Part A: Use

3. What Lowscore is

  • Lowscore is made up of a board (a web page on an old phone or tablet, or an e-paper board), a join page, the iPhone shortcut, the Android app and our server.
  • The phones send scroll time: minutes in the apps the household tracks. The board scores each player against their own normal level (par). The game rules are at the rules page.
  • The numbers are estimates. On iPhones they are based on signals from the Shortcuts app, and some signals can get lost. If no close signal arrives, we end the session after 30 minutes. On Android we use the phone's usage statistics. Differences of a few minutes from the phone's own numbers are normal.
  • Lowscore depends on Apple, Google, Wi-Fi and power. If Apple or Google change their phones, something may stop working until we fix it.
  • Lowscore is a game. It is not a medical device or a treatment. If you are worried about your own or someone else's screen use, talk to your GP, the school nurse or someone you trust.

4. Who can use Lowscore

  • Players must be at least 13.
  • The person who creates a board or buys must be at least 18.
  • One player is one person, with their own phone.
  • Lowscore is for private households. It is not for workplaces, schools or other places where someone has power over others.

5. No monitoring: the rules of use

Lowscore is only for people who want to take part themselves. So these rules apply:

  1. Every player signs up themselves, from their own phone, and decides themselves whether roast mode is on.
  2. You may not set up the shortcut, the app or the automations on another person's phone, and you may not sign anyone else up.
  3. You may not use Lowscore to keep track of a partner, a child, an employee or anyone else who has not chosen it themselves.
  4. Parents cannot sign up their children. Young people from 13 decide for themselves and can leave whenever they want. Children under 13 cannot play.
  5. Do not pressure anyone to join, to turn on roast mode or to stay. Nobody who leaves should be punished for it.
  6. Awards and "forfeits" are for fun. Do not use the board to bully, shame or punish anyone.
  7. Use only apps from our list. Do not try to add apps that could reveal health, sexuality, religion, politics or other sensitive things.
  8. Keep player codes and the board link private. Do not share them publicly.
  9. Do not attack, overload or try to get around the security of the service.

If someone breaks these rules, we can close the player or the whole board (section 15). If you think someone is using Lowscore on you against your wishes, tell us at [email]. We will delete you.

6. When you play

  • Playing is free for players. The first season is free for the whole household. After that, the person who creates the board buys the flat pass.
  • You can leave the game at any time. We then delete you and all your numbers.
  • We may change the game rules and the service to make the game better. We announce important changes on the join page or on the board.
  • The software belongs to us or to those who license it to us. Parts of it are open source under their own licences: the list of licences.

Part B: Buying a flat pass

7. What you buy, and what it costs

  • A new board is free for its first season: 4 weeks from when it is created.
  • The flat pass gives your household access to Lowscore for half a year (183 days), for up to 8 players.
  • Price: NOK 149 for the whole household. That is the whole price. Lowscore is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added [confirm with an accountant]. The total price is shown on the payment page before you pay.
  • The pass starts when the payment is approved. If you buy while the free season or an earlier pass is still running, the new pass starts when the old one ends. You lose no days.
  • The flat pass does not renew. Before it runs out, the board tells you. To keep playing, you buy a new one.
  • You pay by card through Stripe. We never see your card number. [Vipps may come later.]

8. How to buy

  • You buy the flat pass on the board's settings page. There you see the total price before you go on to the payment page at Stripe, where you tap "Pay". The contract is made when the payment is approved.
  • Stripe emails you a receipt. After the purchase, the settings page shows a confirmation with links to these terms and the withdrawal form. [Confirmation on a durable medium under angrerettloven § 18: see README, item 7.]
  • You must be 18 to buy.

9. Right of withdrawal

If you buy the flat pass online, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal under the Norwegian Cancellation Act (angrerettloven). The period runs from the day the contract was made, that is, the day you paid (§ 21).

We make it simpler than the law requires. If you withdraw within 14 days, you get all your money back, even if the pass has started and you have used it. You do not need to tick anything when you buy. (The law would let us charge for the days you used, § 26. We don't.)

How to withdraw. Give us a clear message before the period ends (§ 20). It is enough to send the message within the period. You can:

  • email [email], or
  • use the withdrawal form at the end of these terms.

Your money back. We refund you no later than 14 days after we get your message. We use the same payment method you used, and it costs you nothing (§ 24).

After you withdraw, the flat pass stops. The board goes back to how it was without a pass: it keeps working, but says it needs a flat pass. Players' data is handled as described in the privacy policy.

10. Cancellation

  • The flat pass does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel: it ends by itself when the half-year is over.
  • If you want to stop before the pass runs out, email us at [email], without giving a reason. You then get back the whole months that are left: the price divided by six, times the number of whole months. There is no lock-in period (Digital Content and Services Act (digitalytelsesloven) § 33).
  • When you are done playing, you can also delete the board on the settings page. That deletes all data about the household.

11. Changes to price and service

  • We may change the price of new flat passes. A pass you have already bought does not change.
  • We improve Lowscore all the time. If a change makes the service clearly worse for you, we tell you in good time. You can then cancel at no cost within 30 days of the notice (digitalytelsesloven § 41 and § 42).

12. Faults

  • Lowscore should work as described in section 3. Scroll time that differs by a few minutes from the phone's own numbers is not a fault.
  • If Lowscore does not work as promised, tell us at [email] as soon as you can. We will try to fix it.
  • You have the rights in the digitalytelsesloven: to withhold payment, have the fault fixed, a price reduction, termination and damages (§ 19). We cannot contract out of them (§ 3).

13. Refunds

You get money back:

  • when you use the right of withdrawal: all of it (section 9),
  • when you stop before the pass runs out: the whole months that are left (section 10),
  • when the law gives you a right to it because the service is faulty (section 12),
  • when we end the contract without you having broken these terms, or close Lowscore down. You then get back the part for the time left (section 15).

Part C: For everyone

14. Liability

  • Lowscore is a game. The numbers are estimates. Do not use them for important things, such as health choices, money or decisions about other people.
  • We have the liability consumer law gives, including the digitalytelsesloven. It cannot be set aside to your disadvantage.
  • As far as the law allows:
    • we are not liable for losses caused by things outside our control that we could not reasonably foresee or avoid. Examples: changes to iOS or Android, faults at Apple, Google or your internet provider, power cuts or Wi-Fi that does not work.
    • our total liability to you is limited to what you have paid us in the last 12 months.
  • These limits do not apply if we acted with gross negligence or on purpose, nor to personal injury.
  • For players, Lowscore is free. We do our best, but we cannot promise the service always works.

15. When we close or end things

  • If someone breaks the rules in section 5, we can close the player or the board. If the breach is serious, for example someone being tracked against their will, we can do it at once. We then delete the data about the person who was tracked.
  • If we close a board because the buyer broke these terms, we may keep the payment for the period already started, as far as the law allows.
  • If we close Lowscore down, we tell you at least [30] days before. Buyers get back the part for the time left.

16. Changes to these terms

  • We may change these terms. We announce changes that are to your disadvantage at least [30] days before they apply.
  • If you do not accept the changes, you can cancel at no cost before they apply.

17. Law and disputes

  • Norwegian law applies.
  • If you are unhappy, contact us: [email]. We answer within [14] days.
  • If we cannot agree, you can complain to the Norwegian Consumer Council (Forbrukerrådet), which can mediate. The case can then often go on to the Consumer Complaints Committee (Forbrukerklageutvalget). [check]
