Lowscore privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

  • Who is responsible? Vebjørn [surname], who runs Lowscore (a sole proprietorship in Bergen). Ask him anything: [email].
  • What do we count? Only the minutes you spend in the scroll apps your household tracks. By default that is Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, Facebook, X, Reddit and Twitch.
  • What do we never see? What you look at, messages, photos, websites, where you are, your contacts, other apps or your total screen time.
  • Roast mode is your choice. Only if you turn it on does the board show minutes per app and which app you are in right now. Turn it off and we delete the per-app minutes.
  • Who sees the numbers? The people you live with, on the board. And anyone who looks at the board or has its link. Never public.
  • Where is the data? At Supabase in the EU (Stockholm). The website is hosted by GitHub. Payments go through Stripe. We never see your card number.
  • How long? Minutes per day: as long as the board exists. Open/close signals from iPhones: 14 days. Boards unused for 12 months are deleted.
  • You decide. "Leave the game" deletes you and all your numbers. You can also see your data, correct it and get a copy.
  • Age. You must be at least 13 to play, and 18 to create a board or buy.
  • No ads. No adverts, no analytics cookies, and we never sell data.
  • A complaint? Talk to us, or complain to Datatilsynet.

The rest of this policy explains the details.

4. Who sees the data

In your household. The board shows nicknames, scroll time, par, rank and awards. With roast mode also apps and "now". Anyone who looks at the board, guests included, sees this. Anyone with the board's link can see it. Keep the link private.

Whoever has the settings link (usually the person who created the board) sees the player list: nickname, phone type, whether roast mode is on, par, when each player joined and when the server last heard from their phone. They can remove players. They never see which apps a player has used, unless it is on the board because that player has roast mode on.

Suppliers working for us (processors). They may use the data only on our instructions.

  • Supabase (Supabase Pte. Ltd., Singapore): database and server functions. The data is stored in the EU, in Stockholm.
  • [email provider]: the email account we use when we write to you (reminders, replies and news). Receipts are sent by Stripe.

Independent controllers. They decide about their own part, under their own privacy policies.

  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc., USA): hosts the website and the board page (GitHub Pages), and the Android app download. GitHub stores the IP address of everyone who visits, for security.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, Ireland): takes the payment. Stripe is our processor for the payment itself. For things like fraud prevention and checks the law requires, Stripe is an independent controller. Vipps MobilePay may come later. We will update this policy then.
  • Apple and Google: the iPhone shortcut is shared as an iCloud link from Apple, and Shortcuts and Android are their software. They do not get your Lowscore numbers from us. Their terms apply to your phone.

Authorities: only if the law requires it.

We never sell data and never use it for advertising.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

The database is in the EU. Some processing can still happen outside the EEA:

  • Supabase is a company in Singapore. It uses subprocessors in the USA, including Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare. The server functions run in the Supabase region closest to the phone or board. From Norway that is normally in Europe. If you travel outside Europe, a request may be handled there. The safeguard is the EU standard contractual clauses (SCCs), which are part of the data processing agreement with Supabase.
  • GitHub is in the USA and takes part in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. The European Commission has decided that it gives adequate protection (adequacy decision 2023/1795).
  • Stripe explains its transfers in its own privacy policy.

Want a copy of the safeguards? Write to [email].

7. How we protect the data

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Player codes, settings and board keys, invite codes and the e-paper boards' keys are stored only as hashes.
  • The board's link can only show the board. Only the settings link can change or delete the flat.
  • The database is locked. Only our server functions can read and write.
  • Each e-paper board has its own key. There are no default passwords. An e-paper board can only be added with the code on its own screen, so someone who knows only its MAC address can't take it.
  • The board page link carries its key after the "#". That part of the link is not sent to GitHub.
  • Only Vebjørn has admin access, with two-step login. [confirm]
  • If there is a security breach, we report it to Datatilsynet within 72 hours where the law requires it. If the risk to you is high, we tell you too (Arts. 33 and 34).

Your part: keep your player code and the board link to yourself. Anyone with your code can send numbers as if they were you.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know what we hold about you, and get a copy (access, Art. 15)
  • have mistakes corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • ask us to restrict its use (Art. 18)
  • get your numbers in a file you can take with you (data portability, Art. 20)
  • object to use based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • withdraw a consent at any time. It applies from then on (Art. 7(3))

How to do it:

  • Delete everything: tap "Leave the game" in the Android app, or "Leave the game and delete my data" on the join page. We delete you and all your numbers at once. Whoever has the settings link can also delete the whole board.
  • Withdraw consent to roast mode: turn it off.
  • Anything else: write to [email]. We answer within one month, free of charge. We may ask you to show it is you, for example with your player code.

Did someone sign you up without you wanting it? Tell us at [email] and we will delete you.

9. Children and young people

  • You must be at least 13 to play. In Norway you can give consent yourself from age 13 (Personal Data Act § 5).
  • If you are 13–17, you decide yourself whether to take part, and whether roast mode is on. Nobody else can sign you up.
  • Children under 13 cannot play. If we learn that a player is under 13, we delete that player.
  • To create a board or buy, you must be 18.

10. Cookies and local storage

  • We use no cookies for analytics or advertising.
  • The join page stores your player code in the browser on your own phone (local storage), so you do not lose it. This is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so it needs no consent (Electronic Communications Act § 3-15).
  • The settings page remembers your flat's settings link in the browser where you opened it, so you can get back to it. That is strictly necessary too. You can make it forget with one tap on the page.
  • The website remembers whether you chose Norwegian or English. Our visit counts use no storage in your browser.
  • Our fonts are on our own server. Your browser fetches nothing from third parties such as Google Fonts.

11. Changes

We update this policy when the service changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. For important changes, we tell you on the join page, on the board or by email before the change applies.
