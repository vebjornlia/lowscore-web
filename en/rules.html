The rules

Every number is a setting, but this is the default.

  1. 1

    Par

    Your par is how many minutes you scroll on a normal day: the average of your first seven full days. In the first week the board shows your minutes and "setting par 3/7".

  2. 2

    The weekly score

    Each week, the average of the week's seven days is compared with your par, in percent. −17% means 17% less than usual. The lowest percentage wins.

  3. 3

    Days without numbers

    A day the phone doesn't report counts as par plus 20%, and the board shows "offline". On iPhone the daily check-in helps: it tells the board the phone is fine on days you open none of the apps.

  4. 4

    The joker

    Each week, the worst day above par counts as just par. It happens by itself, for the day with the most scrolling that has numbers.

  5. 5

    The awards every Monday

    Lowscore: best percentage under par, the week's winner. Lowest: fewest minutes (needs numbers from at least five days). Streak: most weeks in a row under par. Reels Champion: most minutes in the apps, only among those with roast mode on. It's a joke award, not a punishment.

  6. 6

    The dishes rule

    The flat can turn it off. When it's on, whoever is furthest over their own par does the dishes that week. If everyone is under par, nobody does.

  7. 7

    Roast mode

    Each player chooses. With roast mode on, the board can say which app you're in right now and joke about your minutes, in boiled eggs and Shrek films. It never jokes about anything but scrolling, and it's off until you turn it on.

  8. 8

    Seasons

    A season is four weeks. After each season everyone gets a new par: the average of the old one and the last two weeks. That way it still counts when you get better.

  9. 9

    Honesty

    In doubt? Show your phone. Lowscore is a game between people who live together, not a check.
